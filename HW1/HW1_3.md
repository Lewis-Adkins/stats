# Question 3
$$
P(A) = .375  \\
P(B) = .375 \\
$$
$A$ and $B$ are **mutually exclusive**, and therefore cannot happen at the same time unless we instead flipped 4+ times. this means that 
$$
A \bigcap B  = 0\\
A \bigcup B = .375 + .375 = .75
$$
