2% of the population has cancer. A screening test claims cancer in people with cancer 80% of the time and it claims cancer in people without cancer 9.6% of the time.

# Question 1

What is actual probability that you have cancer?

# Method 1

if $2\%$ of people have cancer then we can say out of $1000$ people $20$ people have cancer and $980$ dont have cancer. Of the $20$ people who have cancer, the machine has an $80\%$ probabailty of correctly diagnosing the patient with the remaining $20\%$ incorrectly testing negative
$$
20 \times 80\%  = 16 \text{ people correctly diagnosed with cancer}\\\\
20 \times 20\% = 4 \text{ people incorrectly diagnosed with no cancer}
$$

We can perform the same operations but with the $9.6\%$ chance of the test claiming cancer incorrectly in people without cancer:
$$
980 \times 9.6\% = 94.08 \text{ people incorrectly diagnosed with cancer}\\\\
980 \times 90.4\% = 885.92 \text{ people correctly diagnosed with no cancer}
$$

we want to know the proabbilaity of someone testing postive for cancer correctly. this is simply the ratio of the number of people correctly diagnosed with cancer over the total number of people who was diangosed with cancer:
$$
P(C^+|T^+) = \frac{16}{94.08 + 16} \approx 14.5\%
$$

# Method 2
To check my work I use Bayes Conditional Probability (BCP). I say the following based on what was given:
$$
P(C^+) = \text{ probabilty someone has cancer} = 2\%\\\\
P(C^-) = \text{ probability someone does not have cancer} = 98\%\\\\
P(T^+|C^+) = \text{probability test claimes cancer correctly} = 80\%\\\\
P(T^+|C^-) = \text{ probabilty test claims cancer incorrectly} = 9.6\%
$$

From BCP:
$$
P(C^+| T^+) = P(T^+|C^+)\frac{P(C^+)}{P(T^+)}
$$
The probability that someone gets a positive test is the sum of all cases weighted with the associated population who has or doesnt have cancer:
$$
P(T^+) = P(C^+)P(T^+|C^+) + P(C^-)P(T^+|C^-) \\\\= 2\% \times 80\% + 98\% \times 9.6\%\\\\
=11\%
$$
plugging back into BCP, the proabilty that I have cancer if i get a postiive test is: 
$$
P(C^+|T^+) = P(T^+|C^+)\frac{P(C^+)}{P(T^+)} = 80\% \frac{2\%}{11\%} = 14.5\%
$$
